The Problem
Carbon markets
are broken.

A biochar producer in Gujarat generates real CO₂ reduction every day. Under the current system, it takes 18 months and $150,000 before they see a single rupee of carbon revenue.

"Millions of tonnes of real carbon reduction go uncredited — not because the impact isn't real, but because the system is built for the wealthy few."
18-month delay $150K barrier Manual & opaque 94% phantom credits
01
Prohibitive cost & time
Verra certification costs $30K–$150K and takes 12–36 months. Small developers — farms, villages, SMEs — are completely locked out.
02
Fraud & phantom credits
A 2023 Guardian investigation found 94% of Verra REDD+ credits may be phantom. Buyers get burned. Trust collapses. Demand dies.
03
No privacy for institutions
Every carbon purchase is public on-chain. Institutional ESG portfolios and competitive strategies are broadcast to the world — a deal-breaker for serious buyers.
04
Broker intermediaries take 40–60%
Ramesh, a farmer in Anand, earns ₹3,500 from a ₹8,610 credit sale. The rest goes to aggregators, consultants, and brokers for doing paperwork.
