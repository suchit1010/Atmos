Dodo Payments is partnering with Superteam India on a dedicated prize track for the Solana Frontier hackathon powered by Colosseum.

“Stablecoins are room-temperature superconductors for financial services” - Patrick Collison

Stablecoins are rapidly becoming critical infrastructure for global finance because they offer significant speed, coverage, and cost improvements over incumbent infrastructure.

We’re inviting founders, developers, and builders interested in fintech to build applications that integrate with Dodo Payments and leverage stablecoins for real-world utility and adoption.

About Dodo Payments
Dodo Payments is the all-in-one engine to launch, scale, and monetize worldwide. Designed for the new generation of digital SaaS and AI products, they handle everything from payments to billing to distribution — all without extra engineering.

Built for SaaS and AI Products:  they enable you to handle one-time payments, subscriptions, credits, seats, tokens, or any usage metric with their optimized billing infrastructure.

Built-in coverage in 220+ countries, 80+ currencies, and 40+ payment methods.

They have seamless APIs and SDKs for fast integration, along with a Sentra AI agent to build integrations by just describing what you need.

Your Mission
Build a Payments or Finance application that leverages stablecoins on Solana, integrating Dodo Payments to offer real-world utility to individuals and/or global businesses.

We’re open to all ideas, but here are some themes for inspiration:

Cross-Border Payments for Businesses — Help SaaS, AI-native, or creator platforms pay contractors and teams globally without wire fees or banking friction.

Stablecoin Checkouts — Merchant-facing checkout flows (POS, QR, payment links) that accept stablecoins for real goods and services.

Region-Specific Applications — Payment infrastructure built around local regulatory, currency, or user constraints. Example: Credible (Cypherpunk 2nd place) built a stablecoin-powered remittance platform for banks, fintechs, and other businesses looking to send money in/out of India.

Consumer Remittance & Spending Wallets — Apps that help individuals send/receive money internationally or spend stablecoins day-to-day.

Programmable B2B Finance — Supply chain payments, invoice factoring, or escrow that settles in seconds instead of days for SMEs underserved by banks.

DeFi-native Applications — Think applications that bridge DeFi and TradFi using stablecoins. Treasury tools, yield on idle/transitory stables, smart accounts for businesses, etc.

Agentic & Autonomous Payments — Payment infrastructure for AI agents to transact, get paid, or split revenue without human-in-the-loop approval. Brownie points for also integrating x402 here.

The ideal submission will,

Integrate Dodo Payments in a meaningful, non-trivial way.

Solve a specific problem for a defined user: merchant, SaaS founder, migrant worker, logistics, operator, etc.

Showcase how Solana + stablecoins beat the status quo for this use case (speed, cost, programmability)

Demonstrate early traction — real users, a live pilot, or transaction volume.
